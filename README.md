# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing employee demographics, performance, and attrition.

## Dashboard Pages

- **Overview:** total, active, and inactive employees, attrition rate, hiring trends, and headcount by Department and Job Role
- **Demographics:** age, gender, marital status, and ethnicity with average salary
- **Attrition:** attrition rate by role, overtime, tenure, travel, and hire date
- **Performance Tracker:** yearly satisfaction and rating trends

## Key Insights

- Most employees are aged 20-29 (range: 18-51)
- Married employees are the largest group (about 42%)
- Hiring dipped in 2016-2017 and peaked in 2019 and 2022

## Tools

Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling, ZoomCharts Drill Down Pie PRO

## How to Use

Download `HR_Analytics_Project.pbix` and open it in Power BI Desktop.
